# Realty Engine — 7-Minute Demo Script

> The goal: by minute 7, the developer in the room is asking "how do I sign up?"
> Not a feature tour. A live demonstration of speed and quality.

## Pre-flight (do this 10 minutes before the meeting)

1. Open three browser windows:
   - `/portal/orchid-developers`  — client portal
   - `/projects/<uuid>/creatives`  — internal creatives page (pre-empty, ready to generate)
   - `/p/the-crest-worli`  — luxury landing page (have it ready to refresh)
2. Have your phone on the table, ringer ON, on the test number.
3. Make sure Bolna, AiSensy, and Brevo are healthy (`/health` should be clean).
4. Open `/leads/<a-hot-lead-uuid>` in a fourth tab — pick one with a real
   transcript and at least 2 WhatsApp messages.

## Minute 1 — The pitch

> "Indian real estate has a five-minute response problem. A buyer fills a form
> on 99acres at 10pm on a Tuesday. By the time the sales team calls back the
> next morning, they have already filled three other forms and signed a
> brochure request with the competitor. Industry data says: respond within
> five minutes and you convert 21 times more than at 30 minutes.
>
> Most developers can't physically staff that. We solved it."

Pause. Don't show a slide. Make eye contact.

## Minute 2 — The portal

Open `/portal/orchid-developers`.

> "This is what every one of your clients sees. Real numbers. Yesterday's
> leads, qualified-this-month, site visits booked, pipeline value."

Point to the four hero stats. Then the funnel. Then the hot leads table.

> "Every row here was generated by us — the call, the WhatsApp, the score.
> Your team only steps in when a lead is hot."

## Minute 3 — A live hot lead

Click into a hot lead. Show the lead detail page.

> "Here's what happened end-to-end. The lead came in at 4:47pm yesterday.
> Bolna called them within 90 seconds — here's the transcript. They were
> interested in a 3BHK. The agent classified the call as qualified. Two
> minutes later, AiSensy sent the brochure on WhatsApp. The next morning,
> Claude wrote a follow-up nudging a site visit."

Scroll through the timeline. Show the call recording link if you have one.

## Minute 4 — Generate creatives

Open the internal creatives page for the Crest.

> "Now suppose you want to launch a new building tomorrow. Watch this."

Click **Generate 10 Creatives**. Talk while Claude is working.

> "Claude is reading the project's USPs, location, segment, your brand voice.
> It's producing three Meta image ads with copy that respects the 40-char
> headline and 125-char primary text limits. Three video scripts at three
> durations. Two Google Search RSAs with 5 headlines each. A display ad. A
> 99acres listing."

The variants appear. Read one out loud.

> "You copy this to Ads Manager, hit launch, and your money is working
> tonight. No agency. No three-week back-and-forth."

## Minute 5 — The landing page

Open `/p/the-crest-worli` in a fresh tab.

> "Every project gets a landing page tuned to its segment. Luxury looks like
> this — dark, sea views, gold accents, by-invitation tone. The plot project
> at Lonavala? Same engine, completely different page. Bright, big price
> callout, WhatsApp button front and centre. The premium 2BHK at Pune? Split
> hero, EMI calculator, book-site-visit CTA."

Quickly open `/p/green-acres-lonavala` and `/p/skyline-heights-pune` to show
the variance.

## Minute 6 — The live demo

Back on `/p/the-crest-worli`.

> "Last thing. I'm going to fill this form right now with my own phone
> number."

Fill the form on screen. Hit submit. Show the success state.

> "Aapko 2 minute mein call aayegi. While I'm talking, somewhere in the
> background, three things just happened: the lead was written to Supabase,
> Inngest scheduled a call in 90 seconds, and within the next minute my
> phone will ring."

Keep talking. When the phone rings, **pick it up on speaker** so the room
hears the Hinglish opening.

## Minute 7 — Wrap & price

> "Setup is ₹50,000 — one-time, includes onboarding, agent voice training,
> landing pages, the campaign setup, and Claude calibrated to your brand
> voice. Then ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh per month depending on lead volume — covers
> all the calling, messaging, content generation, and the dashboard.
> Plus 1% success fee on every closed-won unit.
>
> One operator can run five to ten of you. Which means I can take exactly
> three more developers this quarter. So — when can your team meet for
> onboarding?"

Don't talk after that. Wait.
