# Packaging & Pricing — How to sell this

> The pitch deck you shared sells at ₹12L/month and claims 150x ROI. You don't need to be that aggressive day 1. Here's a realistic ladder.

## Tier 1 — Starter (₹35K/month + ₹25K setup)
**For:** Small broker offices, 1-2 active projects, ₹2-10Cr segment
**Includes:**
- 1 voice agent (Hinglish), up to 500 calls/month
- WhatsApp drip up to 2000 messages/month
- 1 landing page
- 5 ad creatives per project per month
- 15 social posts/month
- Basic dashboard
**Margin at this tier:** ~₹22K/client/month

## Tier 2 — Growth (₹75K/month + ₹50K setup)
**For:** Mid-tier developers, 3-5 projects, luxury included
**Includes:** Everything in Starter, plus:
- 2 voice agents, up to 2000 calls/month
- WhatsApp + email drip, unlimited within fair use
- 3 landing pages per project
- 15 ad creatives + monthly performance reports
- 30 social posts/month + approval workflow
- NRI 24/7 response on UAE/USA/SG timezones
- Client portal with team logins

## Tier 3 — Enterprise (₹1.5L-3L/month + ₹1.5L setup)
**For:** Tier-1 developers (Lodha/Godrej/Panchashil-scale)
**Includes:** Everything in Growth, plus:
- Dedicated AI voice agent custom-trained on their brand
- HNI intelligence layer (LinkedIn intent monitoring, event lists)
- Architect referral network coordination
- Weekly competitive intel report
- Self-improving loop (the Sunday optimization the pitch promised)
- Quarterly strategy reviews
- 1% success fee on closed deals (optional, replaces some of the retainer)

## Add-ons (à la carte)
- Extra landing page: ₹5K
- Extra 1000 voice minutes: ₹3K
- Custom integration with their existing CRM: ₹50K one-time
- Quarterly strategy review: ₹25K
- WhatsApp template approvals on their behalf: ₹5K one-time per batch of 10

## Cost basis at scale (5 clients on Growth tier)
- Bolna: ~₹15K (variable on usage)
- AiSensy: ~₹4K (one number per client at entry tier)
- Anthropic API: ~₹8K (Claude usage for content + classification)
- Brevo: ₹1.5K
- Supabase: ₹2K (paid tier kicks in)
- Vercel: ₹1.5K
- Railway: ₹1K
- Sarvam: ~₹3K
- PostHog: free tier
- Domain + misc: ~₹500
- **Total infra: ~₹37K/mo for 5 clients = ₹7.4K/client**
- **Revenue: ₹75K × 5 = ₹3.75L/mo**
- **Gross: ~₹3.4L/mo at 5 Growth clients**

## What to actually charge for the first 3 clients
Discount aggressively. Get logos. ₹25K-40K/month for the first 3 + case-study rights. After case studies, lock in pricing above.

## Sales pitch (one paragraph for cold outreach)
> "Most developers lose 78% of their leads because the first response takes 15+ hours. We built an AI engine that calls every new lead in Hinglish within 2 minutes, qualifies them, books site visits, and follows up on WhatsApp + email for the next 30 days — fully automated. Our cost is less than one closed deal."
